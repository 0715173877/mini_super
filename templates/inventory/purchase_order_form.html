Purchase Order Guide
Creating Purchase Orders:
  • Pick a supplier — or leave it blank for a walk-in / cash purchase
  • Set realistic expected delivery dates
  • Use current cost prices for accurate costing
  • Add all items needed in a single order
Order Status:
  • Draft - Still being prepared
  • Ordered - Sent to supplier
  • Received - Items delivered
  • Cancelled - Order cancelled
Best Practices:
  • Review stock levels before ordering
  • Consider lead times from suppliers
  • Verify quantities and costs before placing order
  • Update status when items are received
